## Executive Verdict

The strongest **publicly documented** edges are not the mysterious private books of elite HFT firms — those score high on interest but near-zero on reproducibility. The strategies with the best **evidence × persistence × reconstructability** profile are:

1. **Time-series trend following (CTA / TSMOM)** — multi-decade academic + industry corroboration; highly automatable; crisis-alpha narrative with known whipsaw costs.
2. **Cross-sectional equity momentum** — classic factor with CONFIRMED mechanics; known crash regimes.
3. **Multi-asset risk premia stacks** (value/momentum/carry/quality + vol targeting) — diversified factor exposure with transparent construction.
4. **Market making / HFT liquidity provision** — clearly profitable for specialized firms (Virtu filings, industry structure) but **not** independently reconstructable without speed, colocations, and toxic-flow defense.
5. **Medallion-style short-horizon ensembles** — among the highest inferred quality in public lore, but internals are UNKNOWN; treat as research inspiration, not a blueprint.

## Strategies To Avoid (Weak Evidence / Fragile)

See `databases/07_strategy_failures.json`. Headline lessons:

- **Alameda Research (historical)**: Opaque risk, correlated crypto basis/lending exposures, and governance failures — not a model to replicate
- **Three Arrows Capital (historical)**: Leverage + illiquid venture-like crypto bets + basis trades can fail violently in liquidity shocks
- **Short-vol ETP structures (XIV-style)**: Harvesting vol premium via products with convex liability can terminate in a single shock
- **LTCM-style relative value leverage lesson (historical)**: High Sharpe RV with huge leverage fails when liquidity and correlations regime-shift

Common failure diagnostics applied in-database:

- Backtest-only claims
- Hyperopt/curve-fit indicator stacks
- Ignored fees/slippage/latency
- Leverage dependence
- Survivorship and look-ahead bias
- Short-vol convexity blindness
