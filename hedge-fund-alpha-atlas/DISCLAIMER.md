# Research Ethics & Disclaimer

This project uses **public information only**.

It does not:

- Claim access to proprietary trading code of private firms
- Guarantee profitability of any strategy
- Constitute investment, legal, or tax advice

Missing facts are labeled `UNKNOWN`. Estimates and inferences are explicitly labeled.

Readers should independently verify sources before making research or capital decisions.
